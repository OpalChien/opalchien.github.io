A Place to Keep the Thoughts Behind the Work

Some things deserve to live beyond the terminal. Keep a record of questions, decisions, and lessons learned.

NotesLearning

Sample article · Written to demonstrate the reading layout; not an original publication by the author.

Between research and development are details that rarely appear in the final result: why a split changed, how an orientation error was found, or why an experiment was paused. The process is worth keeping.

Write for the next time you face the problem

A note need not be long. Describe the environment, how you identified the cause, what you changed, and how you checked the outcome. With enough context, it is easier to reconnect with the reasoning later.
