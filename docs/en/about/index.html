A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Opal.

Senior AI Engineer · Medical Imaging · System Integration

Hi, I'm Sung-Hung Chien. You can call me Opal.

I am a Senior AI Engineer at Shin Kong Wu Ho-Su Memorial Hospital, working on radiology AI, deep learning, and system integration. My work includes lesion detection and organ segmentation for X-ray, CT, and MRI.

My background spans mechanical engineering, medical products, and artificial intelligence. From process integration and equipment design to medical IT, model training, and Edge AI deployment, I consider algorithms alongside hardware and the settings in which they are used.

During my master's studies in Artificial Intelligence in Medicine at Taipei Medical University, I researched lightweight deep learning models for non-small cell lung cancer pathology image classification. I also worked on using chest X-rays to help predict significant coronary artery stenosis.

This site is where I collect implementation details, research methods, and questions that arise when connecting AI to medical workflows. Outside work, I enjoy fitness, cooking, and ball sports.
